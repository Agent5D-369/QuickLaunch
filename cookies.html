Your choice

Effective October 27, 2026. Use Cookie settings in the footer to accept or reject analytics at any time. Rejecting analytics does not prevent an inquiry or use of the diagnostic. Closing the settings without saving does not change your choice. With JavaScript disabled, the site does not load analytics.

The site also keeps analytics off when your browser supplies Global Privacy Control or a recognized Do Not Track preference. These signals do not need to be enabled to reject analytics using our controls.

Preference storage

quicklaunchConsent stores your analytics choice, this notice’s version, a timestamp and an expiry in your browser’s localStorage for up to 180 days. It is used to remember your choice, not to identify you for advertising. Expired, missing or malformed preferences default to analytics off. A material purpose change requires a new choice. If storage is unavailable, optional analytics stays off and we explain that we cannot save a choice.

Diagnostic access

quicklaunchDiagnosticAccess stores a grantedAt timestamp in localStorage after you request diagnostic access. The site checks it for up to 30 days. It does not contain the quiz answers. Use Reset diagnostic access on this page to remove it; you may then request access again. This storage supports the feature you requested and is separate from analytics permission. If browser storage is blocked, we allow the local diagnostic without requiring a repeated submission.

Optional Google Analytics

If you opt in, Google Analytics receives visit and predefined interaction events to help understand website use. The measurement property is G-NPCCGNX5FQ. Its first-party identifiers normally include _ga and _ga_NPCCGNX5FQ; exact identifiers can change with provider updates. Our code sets cookie_expires to 180 days and excludes advertising-personalization signals and Google signals. Cookie lifetimes can refresh with use and remain subject to browser limits.

Our event code strips URL query strings and fragments and does not pass names, emails, form text or diagnostic answers to analytics. Consent is checked before the Google script loads and before our event calls. No pre-consent events are queued for later replay. We do not load Microsoft Clarity or session replay.

Withdrawing consent stops optional measurement, clears relevant first-party analytics cookies within our control, and reloads the page to unload the provider script. It does not retroactively delete provider reports already lawfully received. Contact us for applicable deletion requests. See Google’s privacy information.

Delivery and form services

The site serves its fonts locally. Website hosting and ordinary requests can still reveal technical information such as IP address, browser and resource requested. Submitting a form sends your entries to Formspree for delivery and abuse prevention. Provider processing is described in the Privacy Policy and Formspree notice.

A form submission requests that service; it does not enable unrelated analytics or marketing. Third-party sites you visit through a link control their own storage and notices.

Managing storage

You can also delete cookies and local storage in your browser. This can reset your choices and diagnostic access. Browser restrictions may shorten storage lifetimes. Questions or requests: systems@saberra.com.
