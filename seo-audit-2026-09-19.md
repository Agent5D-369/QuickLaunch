# QuickLaunch Website SEO/GEO Audit

September 19, 2026. Scope: all 20 public top-level HTML pages, sitemap, robots, structured data, mobile behavior, conversion wiring, and buyer-language fit.

## Executive Finding

The site is crawlable and has distinct titles, descriptions, canonicals, and H1s. The best immediate improvement is making the highest-intent pages answer concrete operator and founder questions with original examples, clear offer boundaries, and an obvious next step. There is no defensible promise of top rankings or AI-answer inclusion.

- Founder services: `startup-consultant.html`, `founder-advisor.html`, `mvp-validation-consultant.html`, `fractional-coo-consultant.html`, `ai-business-consultant.html`.

## Remaining Risks

- Several legacy guides and service pages are short (about 150-210 words in this audit). Expand with original examples and evidence, not generic filler.
- The six AI pages have similar templates and need differentiated proof before further expansion.
- Existing homepage proof numbers and testimonials were preserved. Verify claims, attribution, and consent before amplifying them.
- Google/Bing ownership, sitemap submission, and indexing requests require authenticated domain-owner accounts; this release prepares the site but does not certify those external steps.
- Search volume, rankings, and conversions require Search Console and analytics access. The query map is a hypothesis until validated with those reports.
- The legacy `mirror/` archive is disallowed in robots.txt but may still be addressable. Monitor index coverage before any removal or redirect plan.
- Large image files, especially Path PNGs, remain a load-performance opportunity. Compress only after visual review and asset-reference checks.

## Technical Baseline

The sitemap contains 19 canonical URLs. The diagnostic URL is intentionally excluded. Public-page browser audit found no JavaScript errors at 375px before these edits. Formspree actions and success redirects were not changed. Google says standard search fundamentals apply to AI features: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features . Buyer-language sources are in `seo-content-cluster-plan.md`.
